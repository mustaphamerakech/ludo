How to play
  • Each player has 4 pawns: 2 start on the board, 2 wait at home.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out. A 6 gives another roll.
  • Land on an opponent to send it home: +1 point and another roll.
  • Bring a pawn to the centre: +1 point. First to 3 points wins.
  • The star squares (both start squares) are safe.
  • 3 items wait in the yellow and red arms. Stop on one to take it; a new one appears at once. Double doubles your next roll, Choose lets you pick the number, Shield protects one pawn from one capture.
  • You have 15 seconds per action, then the game plays for you.
